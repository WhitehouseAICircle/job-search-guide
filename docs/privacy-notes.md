# Privacy before you start

[Back to the guide](../README.md) · [Screenshot guidance](../images/README.md)

Job-search documents can contain more personal information than a task needs. Start with fictional examples, especially for a group demonstration.

## Before uploading or pasting anything

1. Make a separate copy of the document.
2. Remove unnecessary names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth, signatures and identifying links.
3. Remove referee details and other people's information. Do not include identity documents, National Insurance numbers, bank details, passwords or account credentials.
4. Remove confidential workplace information, customer records and material you do not have permission to share.
5. Check comments, tracked changes, document properties, headers and footers as well as the visible text.
6. Share only the section needed for the question.

For example, replace a name with **Candidate A**, an employer with **Retail employer**, and an exact address with **local area**. A rare job title or unusual combination of details may still identify someone: removing a name alone is not a guarantee of anonymity.

For a public demo, make the whole profile fictional rather than adapting a neighbour's real CV.

## Your ChatGPT workspace

Information supplied to a cloud AI service may be processed or stored by the provider. Before uploading sensitive material, review the service's current privacy information and the privacy and data-control settings for your account or workplace. If you are unsure, use fictional text or ask a general question without uploading the document.

Do not assume that a Project is a confidential vault. Check who can access a Project before adding material, and avoid sharing a Project or chat containing personal job-search information. Follow your workplace's rules for any work-related documents.

This guide does not make promises about retention, training use or deletion. Those details depend on current provider policies and the account or service being used.

## This public repository

Only contribute guides, fictional examples and checked demonstration images. Do not put real CVs, applications, private recruiter messages, identity documents or member information into files, issues, pull requests or comments.

Deleting a file later may not remove earlier versions or copies. Check before committing or uploading.

## Screenshots and live demonstrations

- Use a fictional demonstration Project.
- Hide account menus, profile pictures, email addresses, browser tabs, notifications and unrelated chats.
- Check filenames, sidebars and visible links as well as the main conversation.
- Crop or cover information permanently in the exported image, then reopen it and inspect it.
- Keep original screenshots containing personal information outside this repository.
- Use only images you have permission to share.

Do not upload a sensitive image and rely on someone else to redact it afterwards.

## If something is shared by mistake

Stop sharing it, remove the public item where possible and contact the relevant platform or administrator for help. Consider who may have received it and whether it needs further action. If a password or credential was exposed, replace it promptly through the relevant service. Do not repost the information while asking for help.
